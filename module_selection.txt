Instead of today's UK supermarket refrigeration systems operating open-front display cabinets charged with high-GWP hydrofluorocarbons (such as R404A and HFC-134a) that continuously leak into the atmosphere, all major UK supermarkets will physically convert central cooling packs to natural refrigerants (CO2 / R744 and propane / R290) and fit double-glazed doors on all open medium-temperature display fridges within 10 years, delivering a net reduction of 4.8 MtCO2e/yr.
